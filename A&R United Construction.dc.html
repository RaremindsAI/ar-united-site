Commercial Roof Coatings & Restoration

Restore your roof. Skip the tear-off.

A full replacement is not always the right answer. When a metal, TPO, EPDM, or modified bitumen roof is structurally sound, a fluid-applied coating system can seal leaks, renew the membrane, and add years of service life at a fraction of replacement cost, without interrupting your operations.

Seamless, fluid-applied systems

Silicone and acrylic elastomeric coatings cure into one monolithic waterproof membrane with no seams to fail.

Engineered for your roof

We evaluate the substrate, moisture, and drainage first, then spec the right system for your specific roof, never one-size-fits-all.

Cool-roof energy savings

Highly reflective coatings lower rooftop temperatures and reduce summer cooling loads on your building.

Zero business disruption

No tear-off means no debris, no noise, and no downtime. Your business keeps running while we work overhead.
