Common Questions

Insurance claims, answered

Will filing a claim raise my rates?

Storm and weather damage are typically considered “acts of nature” and handled differently than at-fault claims. We’re glad to talk through your specific situation before you file.

What will it cost me?

In most approved claims, your out-of-pocket cost is your deductible. We work directly with your insurance company on the covered amount.

How do I know if I have damage?

Much storm damage isn’t visible from the ground. We offer free, no-obligation inspections and will give you an honest assessment either way.

How long does the process take?

It varies by insurer and the extent of damage, but having an experienced team manage the documentation and adjuster meeting almost always speeds things up.
