Restoration & Your Bottom Line

Often a same-year tax write-off

Because a coating restores the roof you already own, the work is typically classified as maintenance and repair, an operating expense many building owners deduct in full in the year the work is done. A full tear-off and replacement is usually a capital expenditure, depreciated slowly over 39 years.

The same generally applies to ongoing roof repairs and annual maintenance, so keeping your roof maintained doesn’t just prevent leaks, it can keep more cash working in your business.

Every building and tax situation is different. Confirm how this applies to you with your tax professional; we’re happy to provide the itemized documentation they’ll ask for.

Our Restoration Process

How we restore a commercial roof

01

Roof Assessment

We inspect the substrate, seams, fasteners, and drainage, and check for trapped moisture, so you get a straight answer on whether your roof is a coating candidate.

02

System Spec

We spec the right chemistry for your roof: silicone for ponding water, acrylic where reflectivity and budget lead, with the correct millage for the warranty term.

03

Prep & Repair

Power washing, rust treatment, seam reinforcement, and fastener sealing. Coatings only perform as well as the prep underneath them, so we never rush this step.

04

Coat & Warranty

The system is applied to spec, inspected, and documented with photos. You get a seamless membrane and a warranty in writing, while your business never misses a day.
