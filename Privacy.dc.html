Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

A&R United Construction, LLC (“A&R United,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you request an estimate or contact us:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Property address and details about your project
  • Any information you include in messages to us

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Responding to your inquiry and scheduling inspections or estimates
  • Preparing proposals and performing contracted work
  • Communicating about appointments, project status, and billing
  • Sending SMS text messages, only if you have given express consent (see our SMS Consent Policy)
  • Meeting legal, tax, and warranty record-keeping obligations

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us operate (e.g., scheduling, messaging, and payment platforms), bound to use it only on our behalf
  • With your insurance carrier, at your direction, in connection with a claim
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and standard analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it. You can control cookies through your browser settings; the site remains usable with cookies disabled.

5. Data Retention & Security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above, including warranty and legal record-keeping periods. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect it; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

6. Your Choices

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP to any message
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you
  • Unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link in the message

7. Children

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above reflects the most recent revision.
